- Target-size exports now default to 20 MB.
- Change speed per segment: press Ctrl+R and drag the segment's speed bar, or right-click a segment and choose Change speed… for an exact value. Everything after the segment moves to fit its new length, and exported audio keeps its pitch.
- Zoom and crop individual segments: drag a corner to crop, drag the frame to move it, or zoom with the slider or mouse wheel.
- Exports can use your graphics card's video encoder (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel) and switch to the CPU automatically if it fails. Exports with text, speed or crop can be much faster. GPU exports have only been tested on NVIDIA graphics so far; please report problems on AMD or Intel. You can turn GPU use off for regular exports in Settings.
- Undo and redo now cover text and audio gain changes as well as segment edits, in the order you made them.
- Changing audio gain no longer switches the preview to lower-quality playback.
- Text boxes keep their font size and grow to fit their text. Exports wrap lines the same way as the preview.
- Projects now autosave text-only and gain-only edits, and removed edits no longer come back when you reopen a file.
- Closing ClipPress now saves your project first, including an edit made just before closing. If the project can't be saved, for example because the disk is full, ClipPress stays open and lets you try again, quit without saving, or cancel.
- Cancelling a target-size export while it is encoding now reports it as cancelled and removes its unfinished files.
- New Windows installer with automatic updates: choose Automatic or Ask before downloading in Settings. The portable EXE is still available, but it does not update itself. Install this version once to get updates from now on. Updates install only after ClipPress has closed and saved your project, and keep your settings and projects.
- What's new notes now appear once after an upgrade, and empty notes are skipped.
