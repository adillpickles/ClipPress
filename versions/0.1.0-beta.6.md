- **A new editor:** a timeline with picture strips and sound waves. Drag a clip's edges, and back-to-back clips snap together.
- **Text on your clips:** add text (T) and type right on the picture.
- **Share faster:** Ctrl+E exports with your last settings, Ctrl+1/2/3 in one tap (Discord is Ctrl+2), and the video is copied, ready to paste.
- **Clips panel:** rename clips, tick them in or out, drag to reorder, or duplicate one (Ctrl+D). Alt+P shows or hides it.
- **Safer edits:** kept in ClipPress's own folder, and when a recording moves, you can point ClipPress to it.
