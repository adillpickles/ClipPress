- **Smaller files that play everywhere:** Discord exports are now HEVC, so phones play and save them. H.264 and AV1 are in Settings.
- **Much faster exports:** merged clips, sped-up clips and long 60 fps clips export up to three times faster, and a clip that already fits is copied as it is.
- **Your clips on the start screen:** the newest clips from your recorder's folder, with pictures in Recent too.
- **More editing:** speeds from 10% to 1600%, right-click a clip for its speed or Zoom / Crop, drop a file in to add its sound, and dragged clip edges snap to their neighbours.
- **Sound files:** turn the picture off to export just the sound, an MP3 sized to fit.
- **Settings you can undo:** a ↺ beside every changed setting, and Reset all settings.
- **Lighter and quicker:** starts sooner, about 85 MB smaller installed, and updates restart into the new version.
