ClipPress is in early beta. Currently available for Windows 10/11 x64.

Simplified interactive preview: click the timeline to scrub, press I and O to set the start and end. The real export runs in the app on your machine.

  1. Trim

    Set the start and end with I and O, and add text with T. Turn down a loud track and cut as many clips from one recording as you need.

  2. Target

    Original quality keeps the picture as it is when it can. Or pick Fit a file size, type a size limit in MB and choose a preset.

  3. Export

    ClipPress plans the bitrate, chooses the encoder, and measures the finished file against your size limit. If it overshoots, it tries again.

Enough editing to finish the clip.

ClipPress stays capable without getting complicated: trim your clip, add a caption, fix the audio, pick the size it has to fit under, and export. Advanced mode has deeper controls when you want them.

  • Text overlays for callouts and captions
  • Simple audio fixes when one source is too loud
  • One file per clip, one merged file, or both
  • Presets, resolution and frame rate that fit under your size limit
  • Fast keyboard shortcuts, plus projects you can save and reopen

Too small a job for a big editor.

Opening a heavyweight editor for a ten second trim. Installing a second app just to compress the result. Uploading your footage to a random site to hit a size limit. ClipPress puts that small job in one place. Trim the clip, choose the size you need, export it.

  • Private by default. Your clip stays on your machine.
  • Export that fits under the size limit you set.
  • Finish with a shareable video, ready to post.
