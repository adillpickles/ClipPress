Currently available for Windows 10/11 x64.

Simplified interactive preview. The real export runs in the app on your machine.

  1. Trim

    Mark in and out with I and O. Add a text overlay, pull down a loud track, split the recording into as many segments as you need.

  2. Target

    Keep source quality copies the original streams where the format allows it. Or type a target size in megabytes and pick a quality preset.

  3. Export

    ClipPress plans the bitrate, chooses the encoder, and measures the finished file against your target. If it overshoots, it tries again.

Enough editing to finish the clip.

ClipPress stays capable without getting complicated: trim your clip, add a caption, fix the audio, pick the size it has to fit under, and export. Deeper controls are there when you want them.

  • Text overlays for callouts and captions
  • Simple audio fixes when one source is too loud
  • Straightforward export options, from separate clips to one merged cut
  • Quality, resolution and frame-rate presets that fit under your size cap
  • Fast keyboard shortcuts, plus projects you can save and reopen

Too small a job for a big editor.

Opening a heavyweight editor for a ten second trim. Installing a second app just to compress the result. Uploading your footage to a random site to hit a size limit. ClipPress puts that small job in one place. Trim the clip, choose the size you need, export it.

  • Private by default. Your clip stays on your machine.
  • Size-limited export that fits under your target.
  • Finish with a shareable video, ready to post.
